Meet the Team

Dr. Enna Mel

Implantologist

Dr. Mel specializes in restoring the structural integrity of your smile. With an expert eye for detail, she focuses on permanent tooth replacement solutions that look and feel natural. Whether you're looking for a single implant or full-mouth restoration, Dr. Mel is dedicated to giving you a foundation worth smiling about.

Dr. Tooz Braj

General Dentist

Dr. Braj is committed to keeping your natural teeth healthy for life. From thorough cleanings to early detection of oral health issues, he believes that a great smile starts with a strong daily routine. He is known for his quick procedures and his ability to make every patient feel at ease.

Dr. Ruth Kanal

Periodontist

Dr. Kanal is our specialist in gum health and the supporting structures of your teeth. She focuses on diagnosing and treating periodontal disease, ensuring your oral health is supported from the roots up. Her goal is to provide advanced treatments that save natural teeth and improve overall systemic health.

Dr. Moe Larr

Orthodontist

Dr. Larr is passionate about the art and science of alignment. Whether utilizing traditional braces or modern clear aligners, he works with patients of all ages to correct bites and straighten smiles. He believes that a well-aligned smile isn't just about aesthetics—it’s about long-term function and confidence.
